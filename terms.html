1. How ordering works

Items you add to the cart are sent to us as a WhatsApp message. Your order is only confirmed when we reply on WhatsApp to confirm the items, the total, the pickup or delivery details and the time.

2. Availability

Each product is marked In stock, Made to order or Sold out. This updates automatically every few minutes, so an item can sell out after you add it to your cart. If something is unavailable, we will tell you and offer an alternative or remove it from your order.

3. Made-to-order items and lead times

Made-to-order items are prepared after you order and need advance notice. Lead times are shown when you choose a pickup or delivery time (for example, cakes need about 24 hours). Custom and birthday cakes should be ordered as early as possible.

4. Prices and payment

Prices are in Kenya shillings (KES). Some items may be shown without a price; we will confirm the price on WhatsApp before you pay. Payment method and timing are agreed on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

5. Pickup and delivery

  • Pickup is advised for most orders. Our pickup location is shown on the map on our website.
  • Delivery around the area is free.
  • All other deliveries are charged according to Uber and Bolt rates, which are confirmed with you before delivery.
  • Delivery and pickup times are estimates. Please provide an accurate location and phone number, and be available at the agreed time.

6. Changes and cancellations

To change or cancel an order, message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible. Once we have started preparing a made-to-order item, we may not be able to cancel it or refund it. In-stock items can be cancelled before they are dispatched.

7. Allergens and dietary needs

If you or anyone you are ordering for has an allergy or dietary requirement, tell us before you order. We cannot guarantee any product is free from allergens.

Our products are fresh and perishable. Please check your order when you receive it. If something is wrong, message us on WhatsApp the same day with a photo and we will make it right with a replacement, a credit or a refund, at our reasonable discretion.
